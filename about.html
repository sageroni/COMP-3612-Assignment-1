Why I chose to begin the assignment this close to the due date? Obviously because I knew I wasn't stressed enough.

Our Story

COMP 3612 began with a very simple belief; there is no such thing as "too much money" for a flannel. Founded in 2026 by sageroni, this company emerged from gluttonous desire, uncontrollable wealth, and enormous greed. We wanted to see how expensive is "too expensive" for clothes, while also wanting to achieve an A in COMP 3612.
